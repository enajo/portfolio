02 Review

The three viewing planes

Cross-sectional scans are read along three standard planes. A viewer can also stack every slice into one rotatable volume — that's the jump from 2D to 3D.

Three 2D planes → one 3D reconstruction you can rotate and inspect from any angle.

Note

This is a learning sample for demonstration only — it teaches how a viewer works, not how to interpret a scan clinically.
